# Community conduct

Project participation must remain professional, technically honest, and safe.

- Critique implementations and claims, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose personal information
  about another participant.
- Represent provenance, authorship, test coverage, and security limitations
  accurately.
- Do not pressure contributors to publish confidential or patent-sensitive
  material.
- Report conduct or security concerns privately to <dev@arteractive.co>.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect the project and its participants.
